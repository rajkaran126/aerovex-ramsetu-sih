====================================================================================================
Modern military unmanned aviation operates under four fundamental vulnerabilities:

1. THE "BLIND THRESHOLD" TRAP:
   Conventional engine monitoring uses rigid, static thresholds (e.g., CHT > 230 deg C or Oil Pressure 
   < 2.0 bar). However, at 18,000 ft over the Himalayas, air density drops by over 45%, reducing cooling 
   mass flow. An engine running at "normal" absolute temperatures may actually be experiencing rapid 
   thermal runaway relative to ambient conditions. Static alerts fire only after structural damage has 
   already occurred.

2. ELECTRONIC WARFARE & TELEMETRY SPOOFING:
   Tactical UAV datalinks are routinely subjected to hostile electronic warfare, RF jamming, and man-
   in-the-middle telemetry injection. Remote pilots cannot distinguish between a real catastrophic oil leak 
   and a spoofed transducer signal, risking unnecessary mission aborts or disastrous continued flights.

3. PILOT COGNITIVE OVERLOAD DURING CONTINGENCIES:
   When an engine degrades mid-sortie, human operators must evaluate engine wear, remaining glide ratio, 
   prevailing winds, fuel burn, and terrain elevation across multiple alternate airfields within seconds. 
   Under combat stress, cognitive saturation leads to controlled flight into terrain (CFIT).

4. UNSCHEDULED FLEET DOWNTIME & ARBITRARY OVERHAULS:
   Aero-engines are maintained according to arbitrary calendar or flight-hour intervals (TBO). Healthy 
   engines are grounded needlessly, reducing fleet readiness, while insidious micro-wear goes undetected

A. PHYSICAL HARDWARE LAYER & CAN BUS TEST BENCH (STM32 HAL & OPTICAL ISOLATION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AEROVEX features a physical Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) ground-based engine test bench centered around 
an STM32F4/H7 microcontroller, communicating over physical Controller Area Network (CAN 2.0B) at 500 kbps:

1. Transducer Pin Allocations:
   * Crankshaft Speed (RPM)  : PA0 (TIM2_CH1 Input Capture) | 100 Hz | 1 RPM / bit
   * Manifold Pressure (MAP) : PA1 (ADC1_IN1, 12-bit)       | 50 Hz  | 0.01 inHg / bit
   * Oil Pressure            : PA2 (ADC1_IN2, 12-bit)       | 50 Hz  | 0.01 bar / bit
   * Throttle Position (TPS) : PA3 (ADC1_IN3, 12-bit)       | 50 Hz  | 0.4% / bit
   * Cylinder Head Temp (CHT): PB0 (SPI1 CS1, MAX31855)     | 10 Hz  | 0.1 deg C / bit
   * Exhaust Gas Temp (EGT)  : PB1 (SPI1 CS2, MAX31855)     | 10 Hz  | 0.1 deg C / bit
   * Vibration Accelerometer : PB6/PB7 (I2C1, ADXL345)      | 100 Hz | 0.01 g-RMS / bit
   * Ambient Pressure/Temp   : PB6/PB7 (I2C1, BMP280)       | 10 Hz  | 0.1 hPa / bit
   * CAN Bus Transceiver     : PB8/PB9 (bxCAN / CAN1)       | 500 kbps Standard 11-bit IDs

2. Optical Tachometer Isolation Circuit:
   To prevent high-voltage magneto ignition noise (up to 25 kV) from corrupting the MCU logic plane, 
   crankshaft pulses are conditioned through an optical isolation barrier:
   * Isolator: 6N137 High-Speed Optocoupler (10 MBd logic gate output, 2500 V isolation).
   * Emitter: Variable reluctance pickup signal conditioned via NPN Schmitt trigger, driving 6N137 
     IR LED with 330 Ohm resistor and 1N4148 flyback clamp.
   * Collector: Pull-up to clean +3.3V (R = 1.0 kOhm) routed directly to PA0 (TIM2).

3. CAN Bus Arbitration Message Dictionary:
   Defined in hardware/stm32/aerovex_can_protocol.h:
   * 0x100: Core Dynamics (RPM [2B], Throttle [1B], MAP [2B]) @ 50 Hz
   * 0x101: Thermal State (CHT [2B], EGT [2B]) @ 10 Hz
   * 0x102: Fluid Dynamics (Oil Pressure [2B], Oil Temp [2B], Fuel Flow [2B]) @ 20 Hz
   * 0x103: Vibration & Load (Vibration RMS [2B], Engine Load [1B]) @ 50 Hz
   * 0x104: Environmental (Ambient Temp [2B], Ambient Pressure [2B], Baro Alt [2B]) @ 5 Hz
   * 0x105: Node Health & Watchdog (Battery Voltage [1B], Sequence Counter [2B], Error Flags [1B]) @ 2 Hz

4. Physical vs. Simulation Boundaries (§19 & §20):
   * Physical: Ground-based engine test bench, transducers, STM32 DAQ, CAN transceiver, optical isolation.
   * Simulated: 6-DoF flight aerodynamics, NavIC jamming, 4-UAV SwarmNet mesh, and Ghost UAV projection.
   * Continuity: Both physical CAN and simulation streams feed into the exact same backend ingestion pipeline.

C. FIRST-PRINCIPLES ENGINE THERMODYNAMICS & ANALYTICAL DIGITAL TWIN CORE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The analytical digital twin continuously calculates expected healthy states based on first-principles 
physics for turbocharged 4-stroke aero-engines (Rotax 914 / 915 iS):

1. International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) Dynamic Lapse Rate:
   Ambient conditions as a function of geopotential altitude h:
      T_amb(h) = T_0 - L * h
      P_amb(h) = P_0 * (1 - L * h / T_0) ^ (g_0 * M / (R_gas * L))
      rho(h)   = P_amb(h) / (R_specific * T_amb(h))
   where L = 0.0065 K/m, T_0 = 288.15 K, P_0 = 1013.25 hPa.

2. Turbocharger Boost & Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP):
   Accounting for wastegate servo position, ambient density ratio, and crankshaft RPM:
      MAP_expected = P_amb * (1.0 + Boost_Ratio * (Throttle - 0.40) * (RPM / 5800.0))

3. Stoichiometric Combustion & Thermal Balance (EGT / CHT):
   Expected EGT is derived from fuel-air equivalence ratio phi and manifold pressure:
      EGT_expected = T_amb + Delta_T_combustion * (MAP / MAP_sea_level) * (1.0 / phi)
   CHT is solved dynamically via differential heat flux balance:
      d(CHT)/dt = (Q_combustion_in - Q_cooling_out) / Heat_Capacity_Engine
      Q_cooling_out = h_forced_convection(v_airspeed, rho) * (CHT - T_amb)

E. ZERO-TRUST CYBER SHIELD (12 PHYSICAL & TEMPORAL DETECTION VECTORS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implemented in backend/app/cyber_security/zero_trust_shield.py:
The zero-trust sentry inspects every incoming telemetry frame against 12 physical and temporal criteria:
 1. Sensor Freeze / Stiction: Channel variance sigma^2 < 1e-6 over 15 steps during active flight.
 2. Rate-of-Change (ROC) Spikes: |dy/dt| exceeds aerodynamically/mechanically possible gradients.
 3. Sensor Drift / Bias: Monotonic divergence from expected twin without cross-channel physical coupling.
 4. Out-of-Bounds Impossible Values: Exceeds transducer operational physical limits (e.g., EGT > 1200 deg C).
 5. Timestamp Jitter / Stagnation: Delta-t deviations > 30% from the nominal sampling rate.
 6. Packet Loss / Dropout: High sequential missing frames triggering communication watchdog alerts.
 7. Thermodynamic Contradictions: Conflicting physical states (e.g., EGT = 950 deg C while Throttle = 20%).
 8. Hostile Telemetry Replay / Spoofing: Repetition of previous telemetry blocks with mismatched timestamps.
 9. Noise Floor Collapse: Unnaturally clean signal lacking expected engine mechanical vibration noise.
10. Calibration Scale Jump: Instantaneous gain multiplier alteration indicating sensor ADC reference shift.
11. Phase Lag Inconsistency: Abnormal propagation delay between throttle movement and manifold response.
12. Bit / Parity Corruption: Truncated payloads or corrupted frame check sequences.
